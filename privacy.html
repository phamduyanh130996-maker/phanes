Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Phanes is a private tool run by one person (Pham Duy Anh) for his own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What Phanes accesses

  • YouTube: uploads videos the creator made to his own channel, as private, and sets their title, description and thumbnail.
  • Google Drive: only files and folders that Phanes itself creates (the drive.file permission), to archive the creator's finished videos and their scripts and thumbnails. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

What Phanes does with it

Data stays on the creator's own computer and in his own Google account. Phanes does not sell, share or send this data to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
