Phanes

Personal production assistant for one YouTube channel.

Phanes is a private tool used by a single creator (Pham Duy Anh) to prepare videos for his own YouTube channel: it uploads finished videos to that channel as private, and archives each finished video's materials (scripts, thumbnails, the video file) to the creator's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
